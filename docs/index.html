gig-guard

案件文・クライアントのメッセージを貼り付けてチェック。
Paste a Japanese freelance job post or client message. Nothing is sent to any server: the checker runs in this tab (Python via Pyodide), and Gemma runs on your machine through Ollama.

Python を読み込み中… / loading Python…




ローカルの Gemma をつなぐ / Connect your local Gemma

Without Gemma you get the rule checks and the pay math. To add Gemma's grounded findings, run Ollama on this computer and allow this page as an origin:


ollama pull gemma4:e4b
OLLAMA_ORIGINS=https://honru09.github.io ollama serve


The page then calls http://127.0.0.1:11434 directly from your browser. Your browser may ask whether this site may access devices on your local network: that request goes to your own machine, not to the internet.
